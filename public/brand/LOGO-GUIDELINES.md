# Shanghai Crypto logo

The symbol compresses Shanghai's stepped skyline into four structural strokes.
The small red sun/locator point gives the mark a clear local anchor without
using coins, globes, chain links or copied cultural motifs.

- Minimum symbol size: 24 px digital, 8 mm print
- Safe space: keep one-quarter of the symbol width clear on every side
- Use `symbol-light` on light surfaces and `symbol-dark` on very dark surfaces
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor individual buildings or add gradients
- The wordmark spelling must remain `Shanghai Crypto`
