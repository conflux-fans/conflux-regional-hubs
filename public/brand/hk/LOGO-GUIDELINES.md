# Conflux HongKong Community logo system

The mark is **the Crossing**. The open ring is the community's orbit — continuous
but never closed, because participation is what closes it. The swell running
through the ring is Victoria Harbour, drawn as a single calm crossing with a
shorter echo behind it, the way the Star Ferry repeats its route between two
shores. The coral node in the ring's opening is the harbor light — each new
member, each new node, arriving to complete the loop.

Its kinship with Conflux is one of rhythm and rounded geometry only; no shape
is copied, and there is no coin, globe, or chain-link. It is a community mark
and must not be presented as an official Conflux endorsement.

The final wordmark spelling is exactly **Conflux HongKong Community** — always
"HongKong" as one word in the wordmark.

## Palette

| Role | Light surfaces | Dark surfaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `#087e8b` | `#b8f2e6` |
| Accent (node, echo wave) | `#ff9f68` | `#ff9f68` |
| Wordmark | `#087e8b` | `#eef7f4` |

Brand background: `#eef7f4`. Suggested dark surface: deep teal such as `#063d44`.

## Files

- `hk-symbol.svg` — editable master square symbol, transparent background, for light surfaces.
- `hk-symbol-on-dark.svg` — square symbol recolored for dark surfaces.
- `hk-symbol-mono-teal.svg` / `hk-symbol-mono-light.svg` — one-color versions (print, engraving, reversed).
- `hk-horizontal-light.svg` / `hk-horizontal-dark.svg` — horizontal lockups; wordmark text stays editable (`Helvetica Neue / Arial` stack).
- `hk-icon-tile.svg` — favicon / social avatar tile with optical centering built in.
- `hk-symbol-1024.png` / `hk-symbol-512.png` — transparent PNG exports of the square symbol.

## Safe space and minimum size

- Clear space: keep at least one node diameter (about one seventh of the symbol's width) free on every side of any lockup.
- Minimum digital size: 24 px for the square symbol (favicon, list icons); 120 px wide for the horizontal lockup.
- Minimum print size: 8 mm for the symbol, 32 mm wide for the lockup.
- Below 32 px, use the symbol alone; the monochrome teal version is also fine at that size.
- Do not rotate, stretch, add shadows or outlines, recolor outside the palette above, or place the mark over visually busy imagery — on photography use `hk-symbol-mono-light.svg` over a dark scrim.
