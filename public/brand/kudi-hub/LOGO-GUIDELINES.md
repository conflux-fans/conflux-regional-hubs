# Kudi Hub logo system

The symbol uses four protective blue corners to express a safe hub. The lime pathway through the center represents staking participation and money in motion; the coral signal represents current blockchain updates. Its rhythm is locally expressive without copying a specific cultural motif, currency mark, or the Conflux logo.

The final wordmark spelling is exactly **Kudi Hub**.

## Files

- `kudi-hub-symbol.svg` — editable master square symbol with transparent background.
- `kudi-hub-horizontal-light.svg` — horizontal logo for light backgrounds.
- `kudi-hub-horizontal-dark.svg` — horizontal logo for dark backgrounds.
- `kudi-hub-symbol-1024.png` and `kudi-hub-symbol-512.png` — transparent PNG exports.

## Usage

- Minimum digital size: 24px for the square symbol; 112px wide for the horizontal logo.
- Safe space: keep clear space equal to one inner signal square (one eighth of the symbol width) on every side.
- Use the light-background wordmark on white, `#f3f1e9`, and other pale surfaces.
- Use the dark-background wordmark on `#111617` and other dark surfaces.
- Do not rotate, stretch, add effects, change the color relationship, or place the mark over visually busy imagery.
